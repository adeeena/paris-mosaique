# Paris Mosaïque

**Paris, seen one station at a time.**

Explore how public transport connects the places and neighborhoods of Île-de-France.

## Find a place. Follow the connections.

Paris Mosaïque is an interactive map for curious locals and visitors. Search for a station, explore nearby lines, and see which station is closest to each part of the map.

### A quick tour

1. **Search** for a station using the search box. To find a street or another place, choose **Search addresses** in the results.
2. **Explore** the colored areas. Each area shows the place closest to a station in the transport modes you have selected.
3. **Point to or tap** a station or area to see its connections and distance information.
4. **Find yourself** with the location button. Your browser asks permission before sharing your location.

Drag to move around the map. Use the zoom controls, your mouse wheel, or pinch to zoom on a touchscreen.

## Make the map yours

Open **Settings** to choose what you see:

- Show or hide station areas, transport lines, stations, and closed stations.
- Focus on metro, RER, Transilien, tram, or cable car connections.
- Choose light mode, dark mode, or the same appearance as your device.
- Show or hide the legend, and switch between English, Français, and Magyar.

## Reading the distances

- **Direct** is the straight-line distance to the closest station.
- **By walking** is an estimate along mapped walking routes. It may be unavailable for some places.
- Colored areas show proximity as the crow flies. They are not walking catchments or travel-time zones.

This is an exploration map, not a live journey planner. It does not show departures, fares, service changes, or accessibility guarantees. Check with your transport operator before travelling.

## Location and map data

Location is optional and is requested only when you tap the location button. Address searches and walking estimates use OpenStreetMap community services; those requests include the search text or map points needed to provide the result.

Map tiles and geographic information are credited to [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Address and walking-route results depend on community-maintained data and may be incomplete.
